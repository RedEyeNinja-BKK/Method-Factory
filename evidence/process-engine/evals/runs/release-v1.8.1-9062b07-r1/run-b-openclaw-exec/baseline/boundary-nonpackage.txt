### The Ocean

The ocean keeps a thousand dreams  
Beneath its shifting blue,  
It gathers sunlight into beams  
And carries moonlight through.

Its waves arrive with silver hands,  
Then fade along the shore;  
They write their names upon the sands  
And whisper evermore.

Deep in its heart, where shadows sleep,  
The ancient currents roam—  
A world of wonder, wild and deep,  
A restless, breathing home.

And when the evening stars appear  
Above the darkening foam,  
The ocean hums a song so clear  
That even silence comes.
