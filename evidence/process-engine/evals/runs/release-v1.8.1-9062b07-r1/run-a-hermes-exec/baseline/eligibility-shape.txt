Absolutely. We can shape this into a **shop operating package** rather than guessing whether one persona or one skill is enough.

A useful package might contain:

1. **Shop manager persona** — voice, judgment, priorities, boundaries.
2. **Core skill** — repeatable operating procedure for running the shop.
3. **Specialist skills**, if needed:
   - Product/listing SEO
   - Titles, tags, descriptions, and attributes
   - Customer messages and support
   - Promotion and merchandising
   - Product research and trend analysis
   - Shop audits and improvement plans
   - Content/social media
4. **Templates and reference files** — listing templates, response templates, brand voice, pricing rules, product catalog, and policies.
5. **Testing plan** — a small trial before we treat the package as ready.

To design the right package, please send me whatever you already have. Links, text, screenshots, files, rough notes, or existing prompts are all useful.

### First, tell me:

**1. What shop is this?**  
- Platform: Etsy, Shopify, Amazon, another platform?
- Shop link, if public:
- What do you sell?
- Who is the target customer?
- What country or markets do you serve?

**2. What should the package help with?**  
For example:
- Create and improve listings
- Generate SEO titles/tags/descriptions
- Manage customer conversations
- Plan products and collections
- Increase traffic or conversion
- Handle daily shop operations
- Review analytics
- Produce marketing content
- Something else

**3. What does “good” look like?**  
Examples:
- “I want ready-to-publish Etsy listings.”
- “I want the assistant to act as my experienced shop manager.”
- “I want it to tell me what to do each week.”
- “I want consistent brand voice across everything.”
- “I want it to challenge weak ideas rather than blindly agree.”

**4. How autonomous should it be?**

| Level | Behaviour |
|---|---|
| **Advisor** | Recommends; you do everything |
| **Copilot** | Drafts and prepares; you approve |
| **Operator** | Performs approved shop tasks |
| **Manager** | Maintains plans, priorities, and recurring workflows |

Nothing should be treated as ready for real use until we trial it on actual shop material and review the results together.
