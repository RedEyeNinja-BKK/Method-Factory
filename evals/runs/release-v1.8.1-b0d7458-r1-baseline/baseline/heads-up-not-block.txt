## First, define what “good” looks like

Please send whatever you already have—product links, notes, supplier descriptions, photos, existing listings, brand guidelines, or examples you like. Nothing needs to be tidy.

I need these decisions:

1. **Store platform:** Etsy, Shopify, Amazon, your own site, or multiple platforms?
2. **Product categories:** For example:
   - Herbal teas
   - Bath and body products
   - Aromatherapy
   - Crystals or spiritual wellness
   - Supplements
   - Journals or self-care tools
3. **Listing outputs:** Should each listing include:
   - SEO title
   - Description
   - Tags or keywords
   - Product benefits
   - Ingredients/materials
   - Usage instructions
   - Safety notes
   - Image/alt-text suggestions
   - FAQ
   - Social-media caption
4. **Brand voice:** Calm, clinical, earthy, luxurious, friendly, spiritual, minimalist, or something else?
5. **Compliance boundary:** Should the package avoid medical claims and use cautious wellness language such as “may support relaxation” rather than “treats anxiety”?
6. **Workflow:** Do you want:
   - One listing generated at a time
   - Batch generation from a spreadsheet
   - Drafts for review
   - Export-ready files such as CSV or Markdown?
7. **Language and market:** Which country/countries and languages will you sell to?

```text
Product facts

Claims and safety check

SEO keyword selection

Human review

Platform-ready export
```
