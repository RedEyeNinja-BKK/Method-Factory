**The Ocean**

The ocean keeps a thousand dreams  
Beneath its shifting blue,  
It gathers sunlight into streams  
And carries them from view.

Its waves rise up like breathing hills,  
Then fall with silver foam;  
Across the sand, the tide fulfills  
Its ancient journey home.

At night, beneath the moonlit sky,  
The dark sea softly gleams—  
And stars seem close enough to lie  
Upon its restless dreams.
